Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Dayra is a habit app built to keep your day private. This policy explains what Dayra stores, what leaves your phone, and why. In short: your habits stay on your phone, there is no account, and Dayra does not sell or share your data.

What Dayra stores on your phone

  • Your habits and days: habit names, goals and limits, daily counts, wake time, moods, the one line you write when you close the day, challenge check-ins, and your letter for urge moments.
  • Slip notes: when you log a slip, the reason you tap (for example "Late night") and the time. These power the Trigger Map.
  • Settings: theme, reminders, Disguise, and whether you have Dayra Pro.

This data is kept only in Dayra's storage on your device. Dayra has no servers that receive it. If you delete the app, it is deleted.

What leaves your phone

  • Purchases (Dayra Pro). Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Dayra uses RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, not your habits. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Shield on Android. While Shield is on (up to 24 hours after you reach a private limit), your phone's website lookups (DNS) are sent to Cloudflare for Families (1.1.1.3), which blocks adult websites. Only the names of sites being looked up are sent, not the pages you view. Dayra itself does not see, log or store them. See cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Sharing. When you share a story card, the image and a link to Dayra go to the app you choose, such as Instagram or X. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Shield on iPhone

On iPhone, Shield uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls and Managed Settings) to turn on Apple's adult content filter. This runs entirely on your device. Apple does not give Dayra your browsing activity, and Dayra does not collect it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the 9:00 and 21:00 reminders and, on older iPhones, the wake-up alert. Reminder text never names a habit.
  • Alarms (iPhone) and exact alarms (Android): to ring your wake-up alarm on time.
  • Screen Time (iPhone) or VPN (Android): only for Shield, as described above.
  • Face ID or fingerprint: to lock Dayra. The check happens in your phone's system; Dayra never receives biometric data.
  • Photos: only to save a story card when you tap Save.

What Dayra does not do

  • No accounts, no ads, no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Age

Dayra is intended for people aged 17 and over, because some habits it helps with relate to adult content.

Your choices

You can edit or archive any habit, turn reminders and Shield off, or delete the app to remove all data. Because Dayra keeps no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.
