Common questions

Restore Pro on a new phone. Open Dayra, tap any PRO badge, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Dayra. Android: Play Store › profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play.

The wake alarm did not ring. Allow notifications and alarms for Dayra in your phone settings, and turn off battery saving for Dayra on Android.

Delete my data. Your habits live only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes everything.
