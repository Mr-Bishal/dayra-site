Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Dayra works the moment you open it. Your data stays on your phone.

How does Shield block websites?

On iPhone it uses Apple's Screen Time content filter. On Android it routes only website lookups through Cloudflare's family filter while Shield is on. Dayra never sees which sites you visit, and nothing is logged.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. The orb for that day stays grey, and tomorrow is a fresh start. Challenges count the days you showed up.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time, in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Lifetime is a single purchase with nothing to cancel.
